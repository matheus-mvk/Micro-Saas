# Glossary

- Tenant: contracting company that owns isolated business data.
- Branch: operational unit inside one tenant.
- Carrier: transport provider.
- Customer: shipper or recipient managed by the tenant.
- Freight simulation: estimate record for route, weight, dimensions, value, carrier, price and deadline.
- Import job: asynchronous spreadsheet processing request.
- Audit log: immutable record of relevant action metadata.
